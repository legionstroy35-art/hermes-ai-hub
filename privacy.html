Privacy Policy

Hermes AI Hub

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Purpose

Hermes AI Hub is a personal AI assistant intended for use by its owner.

2. Google User Data

With the account owner's authorization, the application may access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Contacts to carry out requested tasks.

3. Use of Information

Google user data is used to provide the assistant functions requested by the account owner, including retrieving, organizing, creating, and updating information.

4. Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and application settings are maintained in the owner's private server environment.

5. Data Sharing

The application is intended for personal use, not for providing services to other users. Data may be processed by external AI services when necessary to perform requested functions.

6. Access and Revocation

The account owner can revoke Google access through their Google Account security settings.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change.
